## Stage 6: Studio's company demo on the frozen engine (Claude, about 15–18 h)

Studio is outside every freeze; nothing in the engine changes in this stage.

### 6.1 The boundary (first, about 2–3 h)

Studio uses SEVER-DB only as a company's application would:
- **Imports:**
  - only `sever_db`; never `sever_db_native`, `sever_partition`, `sever_demo*` or
    `sever_testkit*`;
  - frozen centroids come from `Explainer.frozen_centroids()`, and x̃ from `explain_route`;
  - Groups made quantises with its own written-out §4.2 (labelled).
- **Binaries:** `sever-verify`, `sever-audit` and Part 3's attack kit run from paths in
  `studio/config.toml`. Studio never runs `cargo`.
- **Data paths:** artefacts, the NQ slice and its manifest, Part 3's register and the Evidence
  folder (optional; the page is hidden when unset) come from the config.
- **The one bridge:** `studio.sh setup` is the only code that knows the repo. It builds the SDK
  wheel (`maturin build --features inspect`) and the binaries into the git-ignored
  `studio/vendor/`, copies the artefacts and the register, and writes `config.toml`.
- **The guard:** `studio/tests/test_boundary.py` fails on a forbidden import, on `"cargo"` in
  Studio's code, or on a path under `crates/`, `python/`, `spec/`, `docs/` or `results/` outside
  `studio.sh`.
- **The record:** DB-111 records the boundary (amending DB-106); nothing in `crates/` or `python/`
  names Studio.

### 6.2 The two back ends (about 3 h)

- **`studio/sever_studio/company.py`, "Acme Notes" on SEVER-DB.** It reuses `Backend`,
  `Studio.predict`, `search_explained`, `tools.needles_of` and `count_hits`.
  - `store_notes(name, notes)`: per note, the engine's placement, log position and the routing
    code's agreement.
  - `retrieve(question, k=5)`: the pinned encoder, then `search`, then `explain_search` (groups
    opened), then `passage()` for each hit. The text comes only from the engine.
  - `forget(name)`: `delete_user`, then the receipts and each group's `n` before and after.
  - `gone_checks(name, words)`:
    - search with her own words;
    - `passage()` of each of her IDs: absent;
    - a byte scan of the database's files for her text and vectors;
    - `sever-verify` on every receipt.
  - `calls`: every real call with its real result (what, a summary of the arguments, the result,
    the time in ms).
- **`studio/sever_studio/ordinary.py`, the same app on Chroma.**
  - `chromadb.PersistentClient(work/"chroma")` with default settings: `add`, `query`, `delete`,
    and the same byte scan of its folder.
  - Whatever is found is shown, even if it is nothing.
  - **Spike first:**
    1. `uv add --no-sync chromadb`, then `uv sync --inexact` (a plain sync removes the
       maturin-built module);
    2. store and delete 3 notes, then scan;
    3. report the result before any UI work.
- **`studio/sever_studio/assistant.py`, Groq** (`llama-3.1-8b-instant`, the OpenAI-compatible
  `chat/completions`):
  - stdlib `urllib` on the worker thread, a 20 s timeout;
  - the key from `GROQ_API_KEY`, never logged, shown or saved;
  - the prompt: "answer only from these passages, cite [n]";
  - no key or an error gives the passages, labelled "AI off";
  - the screen says the question and the retrieved text go to Groq.

### 6.3 The main screen "Assistant" (about 5 h)

- **Left column:**
  1. **You:** a name, notes (one per line), **Fill example**, **Store my notes**. Per note: "stored
     at #10412 in frozen-0042 (routing code agreed)" or "waiting in the holding area: stored, not
     searchable until 200 gather (§7.4)". On NQ most notes wait, because spawned groups have no
     room (`2kn < 3N`). **Let other users write** (`fill_bucket("flush")`) fills the area to its
     flush, with the count shown.
  2. **Ask the assistant:** an answer citing [1]…[5]; each citation names its record, ★ on her
     notes.
  3. **Forget me.**
- **Right side:**
  - the 2-D/3-D map (`MapView`, `Map3D`): her notes ★, the hits lit, the opened groups;
  - below it, the tabs:
    - **SEVER-DB calls:** the live log;
    - **Engine state:** records, groups, the holding area, log leaves and the latest checkpoint,
      from `stats`, `group_sizes` and `latest_log_checkpoint`, refreshed after every action.
- **The Forget-me pop-up** (a `QDialog`), rows checked one by one:

  | | SEVER-DB | Chroma |
  |---|---|---|
  | Deleted | 3 records, `n` 34 → 33 per group | 3 deleted |
  | Search with her own words | not found | not found |
  | The app can still read her text | `passage()` → absent | `get()` → none |
  | Her text in the files, before → after | 0 → 0 (encrypted) | as found |
  | Her vectors in the files, before → after | 0 → 0 | as found |
  | Proof she can keep | 3 receipts | none |

- **Its buttons:**
  - **Verify receipts (offline):** ACCEPT, with `StepList`;
  - **Flip a byte:** REJECT at a step;
  - **Save receipts…:** `certificate.bin`, `anchors.json` and the exact `sever-verify` command.
- **Its true notes:**
  - the witnesses are simulated in v1 (§10.4, L-7);
  - the scan reads the database's files, and the raw-disk result is P4-T1's, cited from the
    ledger;
  - what Groq received is outside the database.
- **Ask again:** no notes.
